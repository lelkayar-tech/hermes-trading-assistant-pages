Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Hermes Trading Assistant is a personal application that synchronizes MetaTrader 5 trading records with a private Google Sheets trading journal.

Data Access and Use

The application accesses Google Sheets data authorized by the user solely to maintain and update the trading journal.

Data Storage

Trading records are stored in the user's Google Sheets document. Google OAuth credentials are stored locally on the user's computer.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google user data with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

The user controls the trading journal and can delete its data directly in Google Sheets. Locally stored authorization credentials can also be deleted by the user.
